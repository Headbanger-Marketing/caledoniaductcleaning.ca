FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

Pricing, certification, timing, and the air itself, covered below.

How often should ducts be cleaned around Caledonia?

Two to three years is the working answer for most homes. Exposure pushes it: open-ground wind, gravel driveways, pets, and any recent renovation all argue for the shorter end. We will scope a trunk line and tell you which side of the range your house falls on.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified through training, and fully insured, documentation available any time. Ontario does not issue a trade licence for duct cleaning, so those two credentials, plus references from up the road, are exactly the vetting we encourage.

Can you show me the cleaning actually happened?

Photographs of your trunk lines, before and after, reviewed together before the truck moves. Word of mouth is how business works in a county this size, and we build for it honestly.

Dryer takes two cycles for anything heavy. Vent or machine?

In this county, bet the vent. Heavy loads, long lines, and spring nests add up fast. We clear from both ends and meter the flow at the hood, and if the dryer itself is the problem, that reading will say so and we will too.

What does the visit involve and how long does it take?

Vacuum set, registers washed, every branch agitated under suction, blower compartment cleared, photographs taken. Village homes run three to four hours, larger rural properties with additions run longer, and we book the time honestly.

The first heat of fall smells off. Fixable?

Yes, and routine. The odour rides on duct-wall residue from damp idle months or the household's history. Cleaning removes the carrier, and sanitizer applied to the clean metal finishes the remainder.

We dust and dust and it comes back. Why?

Because the system is likely pulling dusty air in through unsealed return joints and sharing it with every room. Sealing those leaks is the companion job to cleaning, and out here it is usually the bigger half.

Do you cover the county or just town?

All of Haldimand from Caledonia, Hagersville, Cayuga, Dunnville, and Jarvis to Fisherville and Nanticoke. County roads are our daily route, no access arrangements needed.

Do you serve areas outside of Caledonia, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Caledonia and nearby communities including Hagersville, Cayuga, Dunnville, Jarvis, and the surrounding area.
